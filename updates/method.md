# How the monthly update judges new studies
{:.no_toc}

## What enters the review

At most three studies a month are added to the review itself.

| Verdict | Test |
|---|---|
| **Add** | Causal design, 2023 onwards, full text read; **and** at least one of: it changes the sign or size of a result the review states (by more than a third); it is the first evidence for a country, population, outcome or design the review lacks; its design is cleaner or larger than the study the review cites for the same claim; it settles a question the review lists as debated or unknown. |
| **Mention** | Meets the standard but confirms an existing claim with a similar design: one clause or a grouped citation. |
| **Watch** | Could matter but is not there yet: descriptive, preliminary, or a single result awaiting replication. Read again next month. |
| **Not added** | Off topic for the review or below the standard. Listed so readers see it was read. |

## Does it change the picture?

Each of the review's main ideas gets one status: **Holds** (nothing new bears on it), **Strengthened** (a new country, design or outcome confirms it), **Nuanced** (it holds for some groups, settings or periods only, so the sentence needs a qualifier), **Challenged** (a study that passes the Add test contradicts it). The month's overall verdict is *No change to the narrative*, *Narrative nuanced*, or *Narrative revised*; "revised" requires at least one challenged idea.

## Who decides

An AI assistant drafts the update from the month's entries and the review text. Adrien Joly reads and approves it before it is published or emailed. Proposed changes reach the review text only after that approval.
