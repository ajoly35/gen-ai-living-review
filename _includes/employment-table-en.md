Official unemployment rates, seasonally adjusted, read on 22 November 2026 from [INSEE](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/serie/001688527), [ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peoplenotinwork/unemployment), [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/cps/). Young people: under 25 in France, 16 to 24 in the United Kingdom and the United States. France is quarterly, the United Kingdom a three-month average, the United States monthly, so compare each country with itself a year earlier, not with the others. "n/a": no figure was published for that period. These rates cover the whole labour market: they do not isolate the effect of AI.

- **AI at work, United States.** 40.5% of employed adults used generative AI for work in August 2026, against 37.4% a year earlier ([GenAI Adoption Tracker](https://www.genaiadoptiontracker.com/), Bick, Blandin and Deming).
- **AI hiring, France.** 4,576 job postings asking for generative AI skills from January 2019 to October 2025, 705 of them in Q3 2025; 78,005 postings for AI skills of any kind ([LIFT tracker](https://heclift.github.io/website/en/ai-trackers/diffusion-emploi/index.html), HEC Paris, monthly series built from the Dares JOCAS database; the series ends in October 2025). These count demand for AI specialists, not the effect of AI on other jobs.
