What the counts say

    Kind of evidence, by year

    Field experiments came first (2023 to 2024); exposure designs and firm adoption studies dominate 2025 to 2026; theory and calibrated models grow alongside them.

    Theme, by year

    The field moved from worker productivity to junior hiring, then to organisation and to macro and expectations.

    Which themes get which answers

    Count of sources at each theme and answer. Entry and junior hiring is the one theme where the answer is mostly "exposed or young workers lose"; employment stock splits between losses, nulls and gains; organisation returns "it depends".
